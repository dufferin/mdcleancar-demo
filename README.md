# MD Clean Car — CRM & Marketing demo

Clickable HTML demo of a CRM / marketing platform for MD Clean Car (car-care chemicals, Surat Thani), built on the structure of the [Grandprix demo](https://dufferin.github.io/grandprix-demo/).

**Live demo:** https://dufferin.github.io/mdcleancar-demo/

- Storefront with product pages, cart, discount codes, address book & tax invoice
- Member page (points, tiers, rewards) and LINE OA simulation (rich menu, reorder in 1 tap)
- Back-office: customers / Customer 360, segments, products & inventory, orders, delivery, chat, promotions, campaigns (LINE · SMS · email designer), sponsor products, dealers & car-care (B2B)

All customers, orders and numbers are sample data. State is kept in the browser (`localStorage`); use **รีเซ็ต** in the top bar to start over. Product photos and descriptions come from MD Clean Car's own store and are used with permission for this demo.

No build step: open `index.html` or serve the folder statically.
